The Raven

"A gorgeous rotating collection of art and high quality local hand crafted goods, home decor, gifts, and jewelry." - Andrea Morin, Artist (Odd Goddess Industries)

The Raven Gallery and Boutique is a unique shop that sells art and handmade goods from local artists. The variety of different forms of art makes The Raven the only shop of it's kind. Come explore our collection of paintings, photography, collage, sculpture, jewelry, mixed media pieces, custom made/painted furniture and other oddities!

Proprietors

The owners are painter and decorative finisher, Andrea McKenna, and jewelry creator, Javiera Rodriguez. The two met in 2011 at an art festival and instantly connected. Together they created and ran a two day pop up shop called "Once Upon a Market."

The pop up shop was held in downtown Jersey City three times over a year and a half and was incredibly successful. When told by many patrons that they should find a permanent space, Javi and Andrea began dreaming and planning until they discovered the beautiful space where The Raven now nests.
