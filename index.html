Before You Leave

On April 25th, 2015, Nepal was struck by a violent 7.8 Richter scale earthquake that brought the nation to its knees. While the incident claimed the lives of an estimated 9,000 people and injuring 22,000 more, the earthquake, in its wake, has left hundreds of thousnds of people homeless across the nation. Amidst all this chaos, the Nepal Jesuits have been playing an important to bring the most basic needs to the struggling people of Nepal. They have been conducting projects in some of the rural parts of the country, helping the people recover from this catastrophy. Please take some time to learn more about their efforts.
