Here's a timeline of Dr. Ng's life:

  • 1976 - Born in U.K.
  • 1992 - Graduates from Raffles Institution, Singapore.
  • 1997 - Receives undergraduate degree in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, PA.
  • 1998 - Receives his master's degree in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Cambridge, MA.
  • 2002 - Receives his PhD in Computer Science from University of California, Berkeley, CA. Starts working at Stanford University as Assistant Professor of Computer Science.
  • 2007 - Receives Sloan Fellowship.
  • 2008 - MIT Technology Review TR35 names him as one of the top 35 innovators in the world under the age of 35. Starts the Stanford Engineering Everywhere (SEE) program and places a number of Stanford courses online, for free.
  • 2009 - Receives Computers and Thought Award for his work in Artificial Intelligence. Becomes Associate Professor with tenure at Stanford.
  • 2011 - Collaborates with Google Fellow Jeff Dean, and Google Researcher on the "Google Brain" project, a deep learning project at Google. Among its notable results was a neural network trained using deep learning algorithms on 16,000 CPU cores, that learned to recognize higher-level concepts, such as cats, after watching only YouTube videos. Start the applied version of Starford class (CS229a) with over 100,000 students registered for programs first iteration.
  • 2012 - Starts Coursera with co-founder Daphne Koller.
  • 2014 - Marries Carol E. Reiley. Joins Baidu as VP and Chief Scientist.
  • 2015 - Becomes adjunct professor of Computer Science at Stanford.
  • Present - Currently lives in Palo Alto, CA.

"Ng’s work at Google and Stanford University... is behind some of the biggest breakthroughs [in artificial intelligence]."
